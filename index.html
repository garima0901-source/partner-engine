    30 / 60 / 90

    The targets are my proposal, to agree on together in week one.

    Days 1–30

    Build on Deel, open the doors

    • Review the Deel partnership with the team: what it produced and what the offer is.
    • Ship a /partners page and one standard partner offer, so every new conversation starts from the same deal.
    • Apply to the Ramp, Brex and Mercury perks listings. Brex is already a customer.
    • Open conversations with 2 DMC networks for Europe and Asia coverage.
    • Tag partner-sourced leads in the CRM from day one.
    Days 31–60

    First deals live

    • Deel offer placed in Deel Rewards; Remote or Oyster offer live.
    • One community activation with Chiefs of Staff or executive assistants, starting with an event where no competitor is title sponsor.
    • A Preferred-tier agreement with one hotel group in Portugal or Spain.
    • A first Greece or Japan offsite quoted through a DMC network partner.
    Days 61–90

    Make it a programme

    • 10 channel partners live, with partner-sourced pipeline reported monthly.
    • Keep the channels that produce bookings; drop the ones that don't.
    • A written partner playbook, so the next hire or contractor can run it too.
    • A Europe and Asia destination plan with the team, built from DMC and hotel-group coverage.
