# Partner Engine

An independent proposal for a partnerships and GTM function at TeamOut, the corporate offsite platform: who to partner with, what to offer, and what it's worth.

## What's inside

| Section | What it is |
|---|---|
| The gap | TeamOut's own published numbers, a count of its 77 destination pages by region, destinations buyers want that have no page, and five competitors |
| Channel partners | 15 sourced partners across HR and EOR platforms, finance and perks marketplaces, and planner communities, each with a proposed offer and priority |
| Supply | A tiered partner model, 7 DMC networks and 4 hotel groups with planner programmes for Europe and Asia |
| The maths | A calculator built on TeamOut's published booking values and commission; the booking count is a labelled input |
| Market pulse | 6 recent signals (Gallup, Amex GBT, Surf Office's offsites report, competitor activity, the Deel partnership) |
| 30/60/90 | A proposed first-quarter plan |

## How it was made

Researched and built with AI (Claude). Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step, deployed on Vercel. `data/data.js` holds every fact and its source URL.

## Ground rules

- Public, company-level information only. Sources were collected 25 September 2026.
- Figures are each source's own claim. The only assumptions are calculator inputs, and they're labelled.
- Offers and priorities are proposals, not agreements. No partner listed has been contacted.
- Not affiliated with or endorsed by TeamOut or any company named.
